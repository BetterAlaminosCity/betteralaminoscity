---
title: "Issuance of Ownership and Transfer of Ownership of Large Cattle"
description: "Anybody owning large cattle shall register their ownership and anybody transferring ownership of large cattle shall register the same at the CTO"
lastUpdated: "2026-08-10"
office: "City Treasurer's Office"
classification: "Simple"
transactionType: "G2C - Government to Citizens, G2B - Government to Business"
whoMayAvail: "All owners of Large Cattles"
requirements:
  - item: "PIS Registered"
    whereToSecure: "Management Information Section Office"
  - item: "One (1) copy Certification from the Barangay Captain of real ownership of large cattle (original)."
    whereToSecure: "Barangay where the owner resides."
  - item: "One (1) Individual Brand (original)"
    whereToSecure: "Cattle Owner"
steps:
  - phase: "Issuance of Ownership"
    clientStep: "Present the proof of ownership and brand used to the personnel in-charge for verification and schedule of inspection"
    agencyAction: "Verify the authenticity of the Brgy. Captain's certification and brand to be used and schedule the inspection of large cattle and prepare the certificate of ownership."
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "1 day"
    personResponsible: "Adm. Aide VI, Adm. Aide IV"
  - clientStep: "Payment of required fees"
    agencyAction: "Issue corresponding Official Receipt."
    fee: "Issuance of Ownership of Large Cattle: P200.00/head; ICT Fee: P25.00/issuance; Accountable Form No. 53 - Certificate of Ownership of Large Cattle = P5.00/head; Total = P230.00. (Separately, Registration of Individual Brand: P200.00; ICT Fee: P25.00; Total: P225.00)"
    processingTime: "5 minutes"
    personResponsible: "Window 4 – RCC II, Window 5 – Adm. Aide, Window 7 – Ticket Checker I"
  - clientStep: "No action required from the client at this step."
    agencyAction: "Register in the Registry of Large Cattle Book and issue the certificate of ownership to the client."
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "5 minutes"
    personResponsible: "Adm. Aide VI, Adm. Aide IV"
  - phase: "Transfer of Ownership"
    clientStep: "Client present original copy of certificate of ownership to the Personnel in charge."
    agencyAction: "Verify the authenticity of the Brgy. Captain's certification and brand to be used, accomplish AF 52 and schedule the inspection of the cattle."
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "1 day"
    personResponsible: "Adm. Aide VI, Adm. Aide IV"
  - clientStep: "Payment of Required Fees"
    agencyAction: "Issue corresponding Official Receipt."
    fee: "Transfer of Ownership of Large Cattle: P200.00/head; ICT Fee: P25.00/issuance; Accountable Form No. 52 - Certificate of Record of Transfer of Large Cattle = P10.00/head; Total = P235.00"
    processingTime: "5 minutes"
    personResponsible: "Window 4 – RCC II, Window 5 – Adm. Aide, Window 7 – Ticket Checker I"
  - clientStep: "No action required from the client at this step."
    agencyAction: "Register in the Registry of Book and issue the Certificate of Record of Transfer of Large Cattle"
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "5 minutes"
    personResponsible: "Adm. Aide VI, Adm. Aide IV"
totalProcessingTime: "Issuance of Ownership: 1 day and 10 minutes; Transfer of Ownership: 1 day and 10 minutes"
totalFees: "Issuance of Ownership of Large Cattle: P230.00 (or P225.00 if only Registration of Individual Brand); Transfer of Ownership of Large Cattle: P235.00"
---

Anybody owning large cattle shall register their ownership and anybody transferring ownership of large cattle shall register the same at the CTO
