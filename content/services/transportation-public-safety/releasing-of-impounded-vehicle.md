The Public Order and Safety Office is in-charge in the management of Impounding Area located at Brgy. Tangcarang, Alaminos City where all vehicles, including pushcarts and tri-bikes subjected for impoundment and safekeeping, are kept for temporary custody until it has been claimed. This process is provided in order to deliver the simplest and fastest, yet proper way of releasing impounded vehicle to the client.
