A violator of traffic ordinance, other local ordinances and/or executive orders who was apprehended and issued Ordinance Violation Receipt (OVR) ticket or Notice of Violation needs to settle the corresponding fine/ penalty of his/her violation within three (3) days; otherwise, it will be forwarded to the City Legal Office appropriate legal action. Further, for traffic violators, record of their traffic violation shall be forwarded to the Land Transportation Office (LTO) for alarms purposes after thirty (30) days of failure to settle their corresponding fine/penalty.
