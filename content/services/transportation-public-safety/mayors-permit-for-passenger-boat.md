---
title: "Mayor's Permit for Passenger Boat Service"
description: "This service is for passenger boat operators who apply for a Mayor's Permit in the City of Alaminos."
lastUpdated: "2026-08-10"
office: "City Transportation Regulation Unit"
classification: "Simple"
transactionType: "G2C - Government to Citizens"
whoMayAvail: "Renewal/New applicants of Mayor's Permit for Passenger Boats"
requirements:
  - item: "1 original or photocopy of Community Tax Certificate (CTC)"
    whereToSecure: "Barangay of Residence"
  - item: "1 original and photocopy of Barangay Clearance/Certification"
    whereToSecure: "Barangay of Residence"
  - item: "1 original Barangay Business Clearance (for Lucap operators only)"
    whereToSecure: "Lucap Barangay Hall"
  - item: "1 original City Tourism Office Certification"
    whereToSecure: "City Tourism Office"
  - item: "1 original/photocopy of Association Certification"
    whereToSecure: "Association"
steps:
  - clientStep: "For renewal/new applications, client presents his original CTC and Barangay Clearance / Certification, City Tourism Office Certification, and Barangay Business Clearance (for Barangay Lucap Operators only). For new applications, client shall present first the approval from the Office of the City Mayor."
    agencyAction: "CTRU Staff shall fill up the Mayor's Permit for Passenger Boat Application Form (MPPBAF) and check submitted documents."
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "3 minutes"
    personResponsible: "Administrative Aide, Administrative Aide I, Administrative Aide IV, Administrative Assistant I, Planning Officer I or Transportation Regul'n. Officer I"
  - clientStep: "Client settles payment at City Treasurer's Office and shall secure the following required clearances: a. Land Tax (new/renewal); b. Account (renewal)."
    agencyAction: "City Treasurer's Office staff shall accept the payment based on the Order of Payment. Issue Official Receipt. Issue Land Tax Clearance. Issue Account Clearance."
    fee: "FEES (Passenger Boat, 1 Boat, 1 YEAR): ICT Php 25.00; MAYOR'S PERMIT Php 100.00; INSPECTION FEE Php 200.00; LAND TAX CLEARANCE Php 50.00; ACCOUNT CLEARANCE Php 50.00; STICKER FEE Php 65.00; TOTAL Php 490.00. Penalty (25% of the Mayor's Permit [Php 100]): Php 25.00. Interest (2% of the Mayor's Permit and Penalty Fees for every month of delay): Php 2.50 per month per boat."
    processingTime: "Not specified in the Citizen's Charter"
    personResponsible: "City Treasurer's Office"
  - clientStep: "Client returns to CTRU Office for further processing."
    agencyAction: "CTRU Staff shall record in the log book."
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "2 minutes"
    personResponsible: "Administrative Aide, Administrative Aide I, Administrative Aide IV, Administrative Assistant I, Planning Officer I or Transportation Regul'n. Officer I"
  - clientStep: "No action required from the client at this step."
    agencyAction: "CTRU Staff shall prepare the Mayor's Permit for Passenger Boat."
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "3 minutes"
    personResponsible: "Administrative Aide, Administrative Aide I, Administrative Aide IV, Administrative Assistant I, Planning Officer I or Transportation Regul'n. Officer I"
  - clientStep: "Client seeks the recommending approval of his/her application."
    agencyAction: "CTRU Staff/Head shall encode the details of the PB in the Master List."
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "2 minutes"
    personResponsible: "Administrative Aide I, Administrative Aide IV, Administrative Assistant I, Planning Officer I or Transportation Regul'n. Officer I, Supervising Transportation Regulation Officer"
  - clientStep: "No action required from the client at this step."
    agencyAction: "CTRU Head shall sign the Mayor's Permit to recommend its approval."
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "1 minute"
    personResponsible: "Supervising Transportation Regulation Officer"
  - clientStep: "Client seeks the final approval of his/her application."
    agencyAction: "City Administrator shall sign the Mayor's Permit for Passenger Boat for final approval."
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "Not specified in the Citizen's Charter"
    personResponsible: "City Administrator"
  - clientStep: "Client returns to CTRU for the release of documents and sticker."
    agencyAction: "CTRU Staff shall release the Mayor's Permit (owner's copy), documentary requirements and sticker."
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "2 minutes"
    personResponsible: "Administrative Aide, Administrative Aide I, Administrative Aide IV, Administrative Assistant I, Planning Officer I or Transportation Regul'n. Officer I"
totalProcessingTime: "13 minutes (for 1 boat)"
totalFees: "Php 490.00 per boat (1 year)"
---

This service is for passenger boat operators who apply for a Mayor's Permit in the City of Alaminos.
