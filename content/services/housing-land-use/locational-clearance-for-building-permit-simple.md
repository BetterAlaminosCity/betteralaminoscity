Prior to the start of construction, all enterprises and private persons constructing a new building or expansion/renovation thereof are required to secure a Locational Clearance upon application for building permit to ensure that the building is allowed. This covers Group J structures such as private garages, carports, sheds, agricultural structures, tanks, swimming pools, towers, fences over 1.80 meters, and excavation/ground preparation.
