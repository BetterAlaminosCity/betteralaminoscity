Zoning is the division of a community/city into zones or districts according to the present and potential uses of its lands to maximize, regulate and direct their use and development in accordance with the physical development plan of the city/community. Zoning clearance issued upon application of New Business permit to ensure that the location and activity of the business is allowed and conformed to the provision of Zoning Ordinance and Implementation of zoning law or regulation.
