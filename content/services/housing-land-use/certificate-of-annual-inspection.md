Covers the annual inspection and issuance of the Certificate of Annual Inspection required under PD 1096 (the National Building Code of the Philippines) for buildings/structures under occupancy character Group B to J, valid for one year from the date of issuance of the Certificate of Occupancy and renewable yearly thereafter. Single detached dwelling units and duplexes are not subject to mandatory annual inspection, though owners may still request one.
