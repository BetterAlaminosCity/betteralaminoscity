This procedure covers the needed requirements and procedures in the Application for Marriage License for couples aged 18 years old and above who intends and are qualified to get married. Either or both applicants must be a resident of Alaminos City. Both parties must appear personally at the Local Civil Registrar's Office to submit a sworn application for Marriage License
