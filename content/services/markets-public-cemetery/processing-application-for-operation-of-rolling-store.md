office: "City Market and Cemetery Section"
classification: "Simple"
transactionType: "G2C - Government to Citizens"
whoMayAvail: "All"
requirements:
  - item: "Cedula (1 photocopy)"
    whereToSecure: "City Treasurer's Office or Barangay Hall"
  - item: "Brgy. Clearance (1 photocopy)"
    whereToSecure: "Barangay in w/c the applicant resides"
  - item: "Brgy. Clearance - Business to Operate (1 photocopy)"
    whereToSecure: "Barangay in w/c the applicant shall conduct his/her business/ Business One Stop Shop"
  - item: "Rolling Store (photo is acceptable)"
    whereToSecure: "Not specified in the Citizen's Charter"
  - item: "If motorized: Latest OR/CR (1 photocopy)"
    whereToSecure: "Applicant/LTO"
  - item: "If motorized: Driver's License (1 photocopy)"
    whereToSecure: "Applicant/LTO"
steps:
  - clientStep: "Sign in the CMCS Service Request Log book. Present requirements."
    agencyAction: "Receive and review request. Review requirements. If complete, endorse to CMCS Head. If incomplete, advise client to comply."
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "5 minutes"
    personResponsible: "Administrative Aide VI, Admininistrative Aide IV, Administrative Officer III"
  - clientStep: "Present rolling store or show photo of rolling store."
    agencyAction: "Interview client and endorse to inspection staff."
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "20 minutes (for new application); 10 minutes (for renewal)"
    personResponsible: "Supervising Administrative Officer"
  - clientStep: "No action required from the client at this step."
    agencyAction: "Conduct inspection and accomplish the Inspection and Recommendation Slip (IRS)."
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "May vary, depending on location of rolling store"
    personResponsible: "Administrative Aide VI, Admininistrative Aide IV, Administrative Aide III"
  - clientStep: "Return to CMCS Head"
    agencyAction: "Review accomplished IRS and advise client to proceed to CBPLO."
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "20 minutes (for new application); 10 minutes (for renewal)"
    personResponsible: "Supervising Administrative Officer"
  - clientStep: "Acknowledge receipt of IRS by signing the IRS Issuance Log book then proceed to City Business Permit and Licensing Office (CBPLO)."
    agencyAction: 'If IRS indicates "passed": Orient client on guidelines in operating rolling store. Tick the "Recommended for Issuance of Business Permit" box on the IRS, sign the IRS and issue to client. Advise client to proceed to CBPLO. If IRS indicates "To comply with required specifications", advise client to comply.'
    fee: "None"
    processingTime: "Not specified in the Citizen's Charter"
totalProcessingTime: "45 minutes (for new); 25 minutes (for renewal)"
totalFees: "None"

This service involves receipt of application for operation of rolling store (a mobile store, usually a cart, attached to a motorcycle or bicycle, selling food), inspection of rolling store and recommendation for issuance of business permit for Rolling Stores to the City Business Permit and Licensing Office.
